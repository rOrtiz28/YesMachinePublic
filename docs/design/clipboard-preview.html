Shared Clipboard

Copy on one device and paste on the other, as in NoMachine. It's on by default and goes both ways; the computer being controlled can turn it off or send it one way only, from one card in its Settings. Try the switch and the slider: the viewer below follows.

The computer's Settings

The viewer, on the other device

The Clipboard panel says how the computer shares it. The buttons stay, for a browser that can't reach its clipboard on its own, or text you want to edit first.

  • From the computer to you: what you copy on the computer is put on this device's clipboard, ready to paste. On the tablet the app puts it there itself.
  • From you to the computer: what you copied on this device goes to the computer when you come back to the viewer. Safari and Firefox only let a page read the clipboard when you paste, so there it goes as you press ⌘V or Ctrl+V in the viewer, just before the paste.
  • Text only for now, up to the same 1 MB the Clipboard panel takes.
  • The setting lives on the computer being controlled, since what leaves it or reaches it is its owner's choice; viewers follow it. It applies to the next connection.
  • Android devices controlled from a computer aren't part of this yet; that would come after.
