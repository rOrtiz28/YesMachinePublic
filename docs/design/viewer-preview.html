Input available Click the desktop to control it. On a tablet, two fingers scroll. Audio off
  • The cat naps on the monitor. It sleeps on the top edge with its tail hanging down the side, and now and then it opens its eyes. It never covers the desktop.
  • Tools stay out of the way. Keyboard, Clipboard and Files open a drawer on the right and close again, so the desktop gets the most space.
  • Full screen shows only the desktop. A small bar near the top brings back the keyboard or leaves full screen.
  • If the connection drops , this window closes and the main window says which computer disconnected, as it already does.
  • The same look will apply to the page a browser or the Android app opens.
