The rooftop cat .

Background art for the home screen. The cat follows the host's state: asleep when the computer isn't sharing, awake while it shares, and happy when a viewer connects.

Reduced motion is on in your system settings, so the scene stays still.

What moves

  • The cat. Its tail sways and its ears twitch. Every 10 to 25 seconds it turns around to look at you and blinks.
  • The city. Windows switch on and off. The building with ears blinks its two top windows, and the antenna light pulses.
  • The sky. Stars twinkle, and a shooting star crosses about once a minute or two.
  • Sharp pixels. The art is drawn in code at 200 × 76 pixels and scaled by whole numbers, so it never blurs. It pauses when the window is hidden.
