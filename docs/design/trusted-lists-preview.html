Choosing What to Forget

Every Settings row that forgets something now opens into a list: saved computers, remembered sign-ins, trusted certificates, and on Android the computers that are always allowed. A click or tap anywhere on a row grows it to show each computer with its name and a date. Check one, several or Select all, then Forget. Everything in the frames works; the buttons above them jump to each state.

How it works

  • It grows where it is. A click or tap anywhere on a row's top opens its list below, in four steps like the app's other animations, and the computers pop in one after another, as new cards do on the house. The same again closes it. Rows with nothing to list stay closed.
  • A name and a date for each. From now on the apps keep the day each computer was saved, its sign-in remembered, its certificate trusted, or it was allowed, and the name and system it had then. A remembered sign-in also shows its account. Newest first.
  • Ones from before this version have no date, and say "Date not recorded". A trusted certificate whose name isn't known shows its address instead; the app looks for the name among the saved and discovered computers first.
  • Forget asks twice, as Forget all does today: the first click or tap asks again for four seconds. It names how many it forgets, and the notice after says what happens next: a forgotten certificate asks to be verified again, a forgotten sign-in asks for the password, and a forgotten allowed computer asks before it takes control.
