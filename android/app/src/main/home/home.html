Know the rooftop cat

Sarigüeya

The rooftop cat · Puerto Rico

The cat on the roof is Sarigüeya, a real cat from Puerto Rico. She comes from a town in the center of the island, and what she loves most is watching: the cars going by and everything else that happens on the street below.

In Yes Machine she keeps the same watch from the rooftop. She naps while nothing is going on, sits up when a computer is ready to connect, and gets happy when someone connects.

Tap or click her and she’ll send you a heart.

Control from a computer

How a computer with Yes Machine sees and uses this device.

  1. Allow Yes Machine in Accessibility, once. It lets the computer touch, type and use Back, Home and Recents here.
  2. Share from the home screen. Each time, Android asks to record the screen: choose Share entire screen.
  3. On the computer, double-click this device in Yes Machine, then allow it here.
  1. Turn on Developer options: in Settings › About, tap Build number seven times. On Samsung devices it’s under Software information.
  2. By cable: in Developer options, turn on USB debugging, plug this device into the computer and allow it.
  3. Over Wi-Fi: on the computer, choose Add device › Android device › Wi-Fi. Here, turn on Wireless debugging and tap Pair device with QR code.

Use advanced mode?

Advanced mode is the fastest: a touch from the computer shows up here in about half the time it takes in general mode. It works through Android’s developer options, so it needs a few steps first:

  1. Turn on Developer options by tapping Build number seven times.
  2. Turn on USB debugging for the cable, or Wireless debugging for Wi-Fi.
  3. Connect from the computer: plug in the cable, or pair with the QR code in Add device.

You can go back to general mode at any time.
