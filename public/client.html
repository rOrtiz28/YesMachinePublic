First time here? How it works
  1. Open Yes Machine on the computer and allow screen and input access.
  2. Connect both devices to the same local network, over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
  3. Open the computer’s HTTPS address on this device, then sign in with that computer’s user name and password.

Your browser may ask you to trust a local certificate first. Open its certificate details and compare the SHA-256 fingerprint with the one shown in Yes Machine on the computer. Continue only if they match exactly.
